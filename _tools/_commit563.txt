563: refactor — purge the auxiliary veto layer; the funnel decides STRUCTURE only

RATIONALE

  Every veto in v5.62 answered one of two different questions, and they were
  wired into the same funnel as if they were one thing.

  STRUCTURAL -- is this ZONE still valid, judged from closed candles?
    VETO_SIZING         zone height outside the 0.1x-1.5x ATR window
    VETO_NO_SEPARATION  no candle strictly outside the W1/W2 band
    VETO_BROKEN         close beyond the far edge -> blacklisted
    VETO_FLIPPED        wide-range break -> polarity flipped
  These are properties of the geometry that FORMED the zone. They are
  deterministic, they cannot disagree with each other, and they are the
  actual strategy.

  AUXILIARY -- is this a good trade right now?
    Front-Run    the InpMaxRR target was touched before entry
    Stale 45D    45 market days elapsed since W1 with no entry
    Near-Miss 6D 6 market days loitering within one zone-height of the edge
    Momentum     any of the last 8 bars ranged more than 3.5x its ATR
    FVG          a 3-bar Fair Value Gap against the zone
    Correlation  the portfolio's currency-vector consensus disagreed
    News Shield  an economic-calendar blackout window was open
  These are discretion filters. Each one discards a structurally valid zone
  for a reason that has nothing to do with the zone, and each is a separate
  opinion about "attractive" that can silently outvote the strategy. Two of
  them (Momentum, FVG) even defaulted to DISABLED while still costing eight
  ATR lookups per armed block per bar.

  v5.63 deletes the second layer completely. A block now ages out ONLY by
  being broken, flipped, deleted after a structural veto, or consumed by a
  fill -- never by a clock and never by a target it did not reach.

  otto.mq5 -- HiveMind() now resolves the conflict LOCALLY
    This is the subtlest change in the release. HiveMind() used to compute
    supportVote / resistanceVote / mappedVote and then DELEGATE the decision
    to g_correlationFilter.ResolveBidirectionalConflict(), which summed every
    PEER chart's bias through the correlation matrix (GetWeightedBiasSum over
    TS_Bias_* global variables), then BroadcastBias() republished this chart's
    own vote for the next instance. So the zones that survived on THIS chart
    were chosen by the books of OTHER charts.

    That delegate is part of what is being removed, so the majority is now
    counted from this chart's own live zones: mappedVote > 0 keeps
    Resistance, mappedVote < 0 keeps Support, an exact tie deletes both.

    The rewrite also fixes the inversion the old code documented but
    deliberately left alone: its branches passed BLOCK_RESISTANCE where their
    own "kept Support" log line implied BLOCK_SUPPORT, and vice versa. With
    the tie-breaker written as "delete the loser of the majority" the correct
    pairing is structural rather than a remembered detail.

NOT PROVEN HERE

  Behaviour. No Strategy Tester run accompanies this commit. The probe set is
  static source analysis; the compile gate proves the tree builds, not that
  the strategy is more profitable. Two consequences are UNMEASURED and should
  be quantified before this is trusted on a funded account:

    1. TRADE FREQUENCY WILL RISE. Stale 45D and Near-Miss 6D removed blocks
       that would otherwise eventually have traded, and Front-Run removed the
       ones the market had already run to target. Zones now persist until
       price breaks them. Expect more setups and a lower average quality per
       setup unless the structural vetoes were already filtering those cases.
    2. HiveMind's delete decision changed SOURCE, not just code shape: it now
       reads this chart only. On a 28-chart deployment the surviving zone of a
       bidirectional conflict will sometimes be the one the old cross-chart
       arbiter would have dropped. That is the intent, but the magnitude of
       the behaviour change is unmeasured.

  Also unproven: that the retired-input no-op approach actually round-trips a
  real v5.62 .set file. It follows the v5.52/v5.55 precedent and the probe
  asserts the declarations exist, but no .set was loaded into a terminal here.
