567: HiveMind() tie-breaker -- the last cross-zone arbiter is removed; each chart trades only its own setups

RATIONALE

  v5.63 deleted COttoCorrelationFilter.mqh and, with it, the CROSS-CHART
  Hive-Mind arbiter. What it left behind was a scaled-down version of the same
  idea running locally: HiveMind() counted the mapped direction of every live
  zone on THIS chart and DELETED the losing polarity whenever Support and
  Resistance were both present.

  That is still one zone being deleted on the strength of another zone. This
  commit removes it, so nothing arbitrates a zone against a zone any more:
  each chart arms and trades whatever its own structure produced.

BEHAVIOUR CHANGE (read this before running)

  Support and Resistance zones can now be ARMED AT THE SAME TIME. Previously
  HiveMind() deleted one side during the funnel, before it could arm; that arm
  time arbitration is gone.

  The fill-time guard is unchanged and is what resolves an opposing fill: when
  a position fills against an existing one, the structural flip closes the
  older position (COttoOrderManager SAR path). Net effect: the loser is now
  closed AFTER it fills instead of never being placed, so expect occasional
  short-lived opposing positions where there used to be none.

  Checked before removing, so this is the deliberate design and not an
  oversight -- none of the surviving guards is polarity-crossing:

      CanPlaceForDirection    returns true for the opposite direction by
                              design (that is what lets a flip fire)
      IsBlockedByPrimary      same polarity only
      IsAnyOrderLiveAtPrice   +/- 5 points, direction agnostic
      HasPositionForMagic     dead code, no callers
      virtual arm lock        cross-CHART gv lock, KEPT (see below)

  HiveMind() was therefore the ONLY cross-polarity guard at arm time.

DELIBERATELY KEPT

  The v5.49 virtual arm lock (VirtualArmGvName, gating the placement block) is
  a cross-chart resource lock, not a conflict arbiter. It stops two charts from
  arming into the same price at the same moment and stays exactly as it is.

  COttoBlockManager:612 SetVeto(i, VETO_FLIPPED) is the structural WIDE-RANGE
  BREAK flip -- a genuine geometry veto, not the arbiter's. Untouched.

  Every HiveMind-adjacent input stayed declared as a no-op for .set
  compatibility, as in v5.63.

METHOD

  Byte-level edits again (open 'rb' / open 'wb'), driven by
  _tools/_v567_hivemind.py, rehearsed on a copy of the tree before it was run
  on the real one. Every deletion asserted the exact text at its boundary
  first, and edits below a deletion were located by CONTENT rather than by line
  number, because each deletion shifts every index below it.

  otto.mq5 carries 10 mojibake em-dash sequences (c3 a2 e2 82 ac e2 80 9d).
  All 10 survive: nothing above them was rewritten and the mojibake banner at
  :564 was never reconstructed. Zero U+FFFD, no BOM, no bare LF.
